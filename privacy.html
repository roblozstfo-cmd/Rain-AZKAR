Rain System - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Rain System is a Discord bot that sends automatic Islamic Azkar and reminders.

2. Information Collection

Rain System does not intentionally collect personal information or store private messages. The bot may process Discord server IDs, channel IDs, and configuration settings required to operate its features.

3. Data Usage

Information processed by the bot is used only to provide its features and deliver automatic Azkar reminders.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Discord's own privacy policy applies to data processed by Discord.

5. Data Retention

Configuration data may be retained as necessary to operate the bot. The bot does not intentionally retain personal messages.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information necessary to operate the bot.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when necessary.

8. Contact

For questions, contact the Rain System developer through the official support server.
